04 Details

Small app.
Sharp edges filed off.

Follows your Mac: light, dark, or system

The panel follows your Mac's appearance, or you can pick Light or Dark in Settings. Cards keep your note type's own night-mode styling, so they look the way they do in Anki. In the dark, the panel turns pure black and fuses with the notch.

  • Light, Dark or System, set in the app
  • Cards follow Anki's night-mode classes
  • Pure-black panel in dark mode, fused with the notch

Pick a deck. Review its whole subtree.

The picker shows your deck tree with today's due counts, straight from Anki and already limit-adjusted. Pick a parent to review everything under it, or a single subdeck. You're reviewing under Anki's daily limits, same as the desktop app.

  • Counts respect your daily limits, not the raw backlog
  • Parents count their whole subtree, the way Anki does
  • When a deck is done, the next one with cards waiting is one click away

Quiet by design.

No bouncing badges, no streak guilt, no notifications. One number in the notch, and a green ✓ when a deck is done. FlashNotch never pings you. You look when you want to.

  • No push notifications, ever
  • No streaks, no gamification pressure
  • Just a green ✓ and today's count when a deck is done

No notch? Use the floating bar.

On a Mac without a notch, or on an external display, FlashNotch shows the same due count in a small floating bar. Drag it wherever you like. The panel opens right from it, and you can drag the open panel too.

  • Automatic picks the notch or the bar for each display
  • Position is remembered between launches
  • Or pick Notch or Floating Bar yourself in Settings
05 The small stuff

More, in the margins.

⌘

Real card rendering

Images, audio, cloze, typed answers and MathJax render the way they do in Anki, because it's your actual card.

◠

Hover to expand

A short hover opens the panel and moving away closes it. Prefer the keyboard? Turn hover off and use ⌥⌘R.

⤢

Zoom & resize

⌘+ and ⌘− zoom the card. Drag the bottom-right corner to make the panel the size you like.

↓↑

Scroll to review

Prefer the trackpad? Swipe down to flip, down again for Good, up for Again. Optional, with three sensitivity levels.

M·E

Mark & edit in Anki

Press M to mark a note for later, or E to open it in Anki's editor. Sync with AnkiWeb from the ⋯ menu.

−·=

Optional bury add-on

A free companion add-on, included with the app, adds bury (− and =) using Anki's own operation. Strictly optional.

Quick fixes

  • “Anki isn’t running” Open Anki and leave it running in the background.
  • “One free add-on to connect” In Anki, choose Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, paste 2055492159, then restart Anki.
  • “Anki is asleep” Click Anki’s window once to wake it, then try again.
  • Images don’t load Open Settings → Anki → Choose Folder and pick your Anki2 folder again.
  • Nothing in the notch Use Open Review Panel in the menu bar, or press ⌥⌘R.
