01 How it works

Hover. Flip. Grade.

1

Glance

The pill shows today's due count — limit-adjusted, straight from Anki. A green ✓ means you're caught up.

2

Expand

Hover the notch or press ⌘⌥R. The panel drops out with your real card — images, cloze, MathJax and all.

3

Grade

Space flips, 1–4 grades, Z undoes. Every answer lands in Anki's scheduler the moment you press it.

05 The small stuff

More, in the margins.

⌘

Real card rendering

Images, audio, cloze, typed answers and MathJax render like they do in Anki — because it's your actual card.

◠

Hover to expand

A 250ms dwell opens the panel; moving away closes it. Or turn it off and use the hotkey only.

⤢

Zoom & resize

⌘+ / ⌘− zooms the card. Drag the corner grip to make the panel yours.

↓↑

Scroll to review

Prefer scrolling? Swipe down to flip, swipe down again for Good. Optional, off-capable.

M·E

Mark & edit in Anki

Press M to mark a card for later, or E to jump straight to it in Anki's editor.

−·=

Optional bury power-up

A bundled companion add-on adds bury (− / =) using Anki's own operation. Strictly optional.
