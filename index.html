How it works

Hover. Flip. Grade.

01

Glance

The pill shows today's due count — limit-adjusted, straight from Anki. Green ✓ means you're caught up.

02

Expand

Hover the notch or press ⌘⌥R. The panel drops out with your real card — images, cloze, MathJax and all.

03

Grade

Space flips, 1–4 grades, Z undoes. Every answer lands in Anki's scheduler the moment you press it.

Why it's safe

Anki is the brain. The notch is the face.

FlashNotch reimplements zero scheduling. It drives Anki's own reviewer over AnkiConnect, so your intervals, daily limits, FSRS and review history stay exactly as Anki computes them.

  • Drives Anki's real reviewer — never its own queue
  • Your AnkiWeb history never drifts or double-counts
  • Review-only: card creation and editing stay in Anki
  • Undo maps to Anki's own undo
  • If a card needs more, "↗ Anki" opens it in the full app
"Other notch reviewers clone your decks into their own database and re-schedule them. FlashNotch doesn't have a database." — the design principle the whole app is built on

FAQ

Questions

Does it touch my scheduling?

No. FlashNotch drives Anki's own GUI reviewer over AnkiConnect. The scheduler, daily limits, learning steps and FSRS run inside Anki — FlashNotch just shows the card and sends your grade.

What do I need installed?

Anki (free) with the AnkiConnect add-on (code: 2055492159), running in the background. FlashNotch walks you through the setup on first launch.

My Mac doesn't have a notch. Can I still use it?

Yes — the ⌘⌥R hotkey summons the review panel on any Mac running macOS 15+. The always-visible pill is notch-only.

Do images and audio work?

Yes. Grant one-time read access to your Anki folder and cards render with their full media. Audio plays through Anki itself, honoring your per-deck autoplay settings.

Can I create or edit cards?

No — by design. FlashNotch is review-only. The "↗ Anki" button jumps to the current card in the full app when you need more.

Does it collect any data?

No. FlashNotch has no accounts, no analytics, and no network activity beyond the local connection to Anki on your own Mac. Nothing leaves your machine. See the Privacy Policy.
